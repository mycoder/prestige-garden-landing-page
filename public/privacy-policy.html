Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 October 2026 · Website: familiana.online

Familiana respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website or place an order, and how we use and protect it.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit an order, we may collect your name, mobile number, delivery address, district, area/locality, product and quantity ordered, and order-related details. We ask for information reasonably needed to process and deliver your order.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Process and record orders.
  • Contact you to confirm orders and delivery details.
  • Arrange delivery and provide customer support.
  • Handle warranty, replacement and service requests.
  • Maintain business records, help prevent fraudulent orders and improve our services.

3. Order Storage

Information submitted through the order form may be transferred to and stored in our order-management system, which includes Google Sheets/Google services. We retain records for reasonable business, customer-support, accounting, warranty, legal and record-keeping purposes.

4. Sharing Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share the minimum necessary information with delivery/courier providers, hosting or order-management providers, technical service providers, or other parties when disclosure is required by law or needed to protect our legal rights.

5. Calls and Customer Support

We may call the mobile number you provide to confirm an order, clarify the address, provide delivery updates, or help with warranty and replacement matters. If you contact us through WhatsApp, we use the information you share to respond to your request.

6. Cookies, Meta Pixel and Advertising

Our website may use cookies, pixels and similar technologies to operate the site, understand website interactions, measure advertising performance and improve our services. This includes Meta Pixel, which may process information about activity on our website in accordance with Meta's policies and your applicable settings. You can review Meta's privacy information and manage relevant browser settings or ad preferences.

7. Payment Information

Our current ordering process offers Cash on Delivery (COD), subject to order and delivery confirmation. Do not send us card PINs, banking passwords or OTPs through the website or customer-support channels.

8. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect submitted information. No online transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. We retain information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described in this policy or as required by applicable law.

9. Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services for hosting, order management, analytics, advertising, communication and delivery. Those providers may process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

10. Your Requests

If you believe your information is incorrect or want to ask about, correct or request deletion of information, contact us using the details below. We will review the request and respond as appropriate, subject to applicable legal and record-keeping requirements.

11. Children's Privacy

This website is intended for general customers and is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly request unnecessary personal information from children.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when our services, practices or legal requirements change. The latest version and effective date will be posted on this page.
